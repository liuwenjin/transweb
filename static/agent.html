Frequently asked questions about AI digital labor

What is AI digital labor?
It is an AI colleague that works from a web page: it has a role, a document basis and skills, and it can chain skills into a workflow to finish a whole process, then be handed to a specific person by link, page or QR code.
How is it different from a chatbot or an AI agent?
A chatbot only talks; an agent calls tools. Digital labor is closer to a role — it reads your documents, calls skills and produces results through a workflow, and anyone can open the link to use it.
Are my documents used to train models?
No. Documents are used only to build your own digital worker and are stored isolated per account. Traffic is encrypted over HTTPS, and documents are not used for public model training or shared with other users.
